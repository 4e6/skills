# Invariant

* [Every cooked portion is eaten within the plan](portion-conservation.md) - A recipe's pot must map every portion to a named meal on a day the plan covers, nothing eaten before it is cooked or more than four days after. Held in code, because a plan that over-cooks costs money and trust.
* [Nothing about the athlete is invented](nothing-about-the-athlete-is-invented.md) - No figure about the athlete's week that they did not give may be printed, and a missing required input is a question, never a guess. What they did give is theirs to print and to fuel from.
* [The shopping list buys what the week uses](the-list-buys-what-the-week-uses.md) - Each row's quantity is what the recipes cook with, less what the fridge holds, and its days are the days that eat what it buys. One amount of food per row, never a pack size, and a count rounded up to a whole; a staple is the jar, with no days.
