# The question

A condition treated by leaving a food out — coeliac, an allergy — is a
restriction the plan honours, and the closing line is worded not to turn those
athletes away ([the intake](/architecture/the-intake.md#a-medical-diet-is-a-restriction)).

A condition treated by **how much somebody eats** — insulin-treated diabetes,
kidney disease, an eating disorder — is what the line is about. Today the skill
only says so at the end, under a plan already written.

# The options

- Say it before planning, at step 1.
- Decline to plan.
- Leave it at the end, as now.

A sentence that would have licensed a plan for these athletes on their consent
was rejected. Any option changes the skill's stance, not only its wording.

# Also

The skill's `README.md` still carries the unqualified sentence, which reads as
turning away the athletes the closing line was reworded to include.
