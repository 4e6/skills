Training week

The week · Recipes · Shopping list
Load
6 training days, 1 rest day
Hard days
Sat (TT bike + brick run), Sun (threshold run + swim), Wed (VO2max intervals), Tue (bike threshold + brick)
Easy days
Mon (aerobic run), Thu (swim set), Fri (rest day)

Saturday's TT ride and brick run make it the biggest fuel day, and Friday is fed for it rather than as a rest day. Sunday, Wednesday and Tuesday follow; Monday's easy run and Thursday's swim set get the lightest meals.

Recipes

Monday Breakfast

Porridge with banana, walnuts and honey

prep 3 min · cook 7 min

375 kcal · 54 g carbs · 13 g protein · 12 g fat · 6 g fibre

Makes 0.5 portions.

  • Rolled oats 40 g
  • Milk 150 ml
  • Banana 60 g, sliced (1)
  • Walnuts 10 g, roughly chopped
  • Honey 10 g
  1. Bring the oats and milk to a gentle simmer, stirring.
  2. Cook 5–7 minutes until thick, adding a splash more milk if needed.
  3. Top with banana, walnuts and honey.

Monday Lunch

Chicken and rice bowl with roasted peppers

prep 10 min · cook 25 min

580 kcal · 63 g carbs · 44 g protein · 16 g fat · 4 g fibre

Makes 1 portion.

  • Chicken breast 150 g (1)
  • Rice 70 g dry
  • Red pepper 1, sliced
  • Onion half, sliced
  • Olive oil 1 tbsp
  1. Roast the pepper and onion with the oil at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  2. Cook the rice.
  3. Season and pan-fry the chicken 5–6 minutes per side, then rest and slice.
  4. Combine in a bowl.

Monday Dinner

Lentil soup

prep 10 min · cook 35 min

610 kcal · 93 g carbs · 29 g protein · 10 g fat · 18 g fibre

Makes 2 portions: Monday Dinner, Wednesday Lunch

  • Brown lentils 200 g
  • Onion 1, diced
  • Carrots 2, diced
  • Stock 1 L
  • Olive oil 1 tbsp
  • Sourdough 2 slices
  1. Soften the onion and carrot in the oil, 8 minutes.
  2. Add lentils and stock, simmer 30 minutes until tender.
  3. Season, and blend half for body if you prefer it thicker.
  4. Serve with a slice of sourdough; cool the reserved portion quickly and refrigerate.

Tuesday Breakfast

Eggs on toast with avocado

prep 5 min · cook 5 min

620 kcal · 68 g carbs · 27 g protein · 30 g fat · 9 g fibre

Makes 1 portion.

  • Eggs 2
  • Sourdough 4 slices
  • Avocado half
  • Olive oil 1 tsp
  1. Toast the sourdough.
  2. Fry or poach the eggs to your preference.
  3. Mash the avocado onto the toast, season, and top with the eggs.

Tuesday Lunch

Tuna pasta salad

prep 5 min · cook 12 min

750 kcal · 102 g carbs · 42 g protein · 19 g fat · 8 g fibre

Makes 1 portion.

  • Short pasta 130 g dry
  • Tinned tuna, drained 120 g (1 tin)
  • Red pepper half, diced
  • Olive oil 1 tbsp
  • Lemon half
  1. Cook the pasta, drain and cool briefly under cold water.
  2. Flake in the tuna and add the pepper.
  3. Dress with oil and lemon, season well.

Tuesday Dinner

Salmon fillet with sweet potato and green beans

prep 10 min · cook 30 min

620 kcal · 52 g carbs · 40 g protein · 26 g fat · 8 g fibre

Makes 1 portion.

  • Salmon fillet 200 g
  • Sweet potato 300 g, cubed (2)
  • Green beans 200 g
  • Olive oil 1 tbsp
  1. Roast the sweet potato with oil at 200°C for 25 minutes.
  2. Add the salmon for the last 12 minutes.
  3. Steam the green beans 5 minutes and serve alongside.

Wednesday Dinner

Spaghetti bolognese

prep 15 min · cook 40 min

680 kcal · 84 g carbs · 38 g protein · 22 g fat · 7 g fibre

Makes 3 portions: Wednesday Dinner, Saturday Lunch, Sunday Lunch

  • Ground beef 500 g
  • Tinned tomatoes 800 g (2 tins)
  • Onion 1, diced
  • Carrot 1, diced
  • Spaghetti, dry 270 g
  1. Brown the beef in a wide pan, breaking it up as it colours.
  2. Add the onion and carrot, cook 8 minutes.
  3. Add the tomatoes, simmer 30 minutes, season.
  4. Cook spaghetti for the portion you are eating now; cool and refrigerate the rest of the sauce.

Thursday Breakfast

Greek yogurt with oats, berries and honey

prep 5 min · cook 0 min

430 kcal · 58 g carbs · 24 g protein · 11 g fat · 6 g fibre

Makes 1 portion.

  • Greek yogurt 250 g
  • Rolled oats 40 g
  • Frozen berries 100 g
  • Honey 1 tbsp
  1. Thaw the berries briefly, or use them frozen for a thicker result.
  2. Layer yogurt, oats and berries.
  3. Finish with honey.

Thursday Lunch

Chickpea and roasted vegetable salad

prep 10 min · cook 25 min

520 kcal · 62 g carbs · 18 g protein · 20 g fat · 14 g fibre

Makes 1 portion.

  • Tinned chickpeas, drained 240 g (1 tin)
  • Courgette 1, sliced
  • Red pepper 1, sliced
  • Salad leaves handful
  • Olive oil 1 tbsp
  1. Roast the courgette and pepper with oil at 200°C for 25 minutes.
  2. Warm the chickpeas through with the roasted vegetables.
  3. Serve over the leaves, dressed with the pan oil.

Thursday Dinner

Omelette with potatoes and side salad

prep 10 min · cook 20 min

560 kcal · 44 g carbs · 26 g protein · 30 g fat · 6 g fibre

Makes 1 portion.

  • Eggs 3
  • Potatoes, thinly sliced 250 g (2)
  • Onion half, sliced
  • Salad leaves handful
  • Olive oil 2 tbsp
  1. Soften the potato and onion in the oil over low heat, 12 minutes.
  2. Beat the eggs, season, and pour over.
  3. Cook gently until set, turning once.
  4. Serve with the dressed leaves.

Friday Dinner

Grilled mackerel with boiled potatoes and tomato salad

prep 10 min · cook 25 min

680 kcal · 95 g carbs · 28 g protein · 19 g fat · 10 g fibre

Makes 1 portion.

  • Mackerel 1 fillet

  • Tomatoes 3, sliced
  • Olive oil 1 tsp
  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, 20 minutes.
  2. Grill the mackerel 5 minutes per side.
  3. Dress the tomatoes with oil and salt, and serve everything together.
